Borrow a Musical Instrument from a Public Library

A library may lend an instrument to take home, offer it only inside a music room, or handle the two through different programs. Start with the named branch and the instrument you want.

In McFarland, Wisconsin, ukulele kits are first come, first served. Sydney lets members take selected instruments home, while guitars and the grand piano at Green Square are offered through a separate music room hire.

What to check before you pick it up

Ask whether a hold means you can collect an instrument now or join a waiting list. Confirm the due date and where the loan has to be returned.

For a school project or practice at home, check the size of the instrument and whether the loan includes a case, tuner or power supply. Use the organizer’s own catalogue for current availability.
