How to check in

Check the event listing before you leave. Confirm whether you need to sign up, when intake closes and which item categories volunteers will handle.

  • Repair Café San Francisco

    San Francisco, California, US

    Event
    Use the organizer’s current listing. The venue can change. San Francisco visitor FAQ
    Parts
    The organizer keeps a limited supply of spare parts. San Francisco visitor FAQ

    San Francisco visitor FAQ

  • Repair Café of the Peninsula

    San Mateo County, California, US

    Check-in
    Regular events use drop-in check-in. Expect a waiver and an item intake form. Peninsula visitor FAQ
    Item count
    The organizer asks visitors to bring no more than two items each. Check the specific event for its item categories. Peninsula visitor FAQ

    Peninsula upcoming events

  • Repair Café Toronto

    Toronto, Ontario, Canada

    Check-in
    Sign up on arrival. Intake closes one hour before the event ends. Toronto visitor guide
    Item count
    During busy periods, volunteers examine one item per person. Toronto visitor guide
    Parts
    The visitor guide says the café currently cannot supply replacement parts. Toronto visitor guide

    Toronto upcoming events

  • York Region Repair Cafés

    York Region, Ontario, Canada

    Check-in
    York Region residents can register ahead for an appointment. The operator lists registration by event. York Region dates and program rules
    Parts
    Bring any replacement part you already know the item needs. York Region dates and program rules

    York Region dates and program rules

  • Repair Café Wales

    Wales, United Kingdom

    Check-in
    Visitors are seen in arrival order. Sign the repair form and plan to stay with your item. Wales visitor FAQ · Wales house rules
    Item count
    When the event is busy, one item per person is examined. Late arrivals may be turned away in the final hour. Wales house rules
    Parts
    Visitors pay separately for any new materials used. Wales house rules

    Wales events

  • Rice County Fix-It Clinics

    Rice County, Minnesota, US

    Item count
    Bring up to two items you can carry in. The county excludes oversized items. Rice County clinic guide and dates
    Event
    Use the current county listing for the clinic venue and date. Rice County clinic guide and dates

    Rice County clinic guide and dates

  • Waverley Council Repair Café

    Waverley, New South Wales, Australia

    Check-in
    Check in on arrival. A volunteer gives you a time slot after intake, in arrival order. Advance booking is unavailable. Waverley Council repair café guide
    Size
    Bring an item one person can carry. The council describes a 30-minute repair slot. Waverley Council repair café guide

    Waverley Council repair café guide

Using these rules

Event listings can limit what volunteers consider on the day. A listed item can still be declined. Each comparison links to the organizer’s published rules.
