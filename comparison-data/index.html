How to read a record

record_id and answer_url
Use the stable ID for joins and the link to open the exact task and operator.
guide, operator_id, item_id and jurisdiction
Apply each rule only to the named guide, program, task and place. Match on these IDs before comparing findings.
fit and rule
Listed means there is a published statement for the task. Excluded means the operator expressly restricts it. Ask means confirm first. Paused indicates the operator is not currently asking for an item. Unknown means the reviewed material did not settle the task.
policy_source, source_checked_on, and organizer_route
The policy source and review date belong to the stated rule. For unknown records, those fields are blank and the organizer route gives a place to check.
snapshot_reviewed_on
The guide’s snapshot date. Use the linked operator for current eligibility, hours and availability.
