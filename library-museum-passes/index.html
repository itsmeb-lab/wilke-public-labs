Do I need a separate ticket with a library museum pass?

It depends on the pass and the venue. Some places use it at the door, and others ask you to book a time separately. These five routes show what the libraries and venues actually publish.

Check availability and the visit date with both organizations. If the venue requires a separate timed ticket, make sure the pass and ticket dates match.

Check how many people it covers

  • Boston Public Library → New England Aquarium

    Boston, Massachusetts

    Listed by organizer

    The Boston Public Library digital pass covers general admission for up to four people on its date. The Aquarium publishes separate terms for coupons from other libraries.

    New England Aquarium pass redemption

  • Shorewood Library → Betty Brinn Children’s Museum

    Shorewood and Milwaukee, Wisconsin

    Listed by organizer

    Shorewood lists admission for up to six guests per pass. Children must be accompanied by an adult 18 or older.

    Pittsfield and North Adams, Massachusetts

Check the pass before buying a ticket

Start with your library’s eligibility rules and look up the exact venue. For the New England Aquarium, choose the Boston Public Library pass instructions if that is your library. The Aquarium publishes different terms for coupons issued by other partners.

At Betty Brinn, the venue’s separate ticket and adult-visit instructions apply. In Burlington County, the library explains how to check time slots first for venues that require a timed ticket.
