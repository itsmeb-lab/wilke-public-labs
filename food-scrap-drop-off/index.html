How to use the drop-off

Check the current location and whether you need to sign up or unlock a cart. The rules below apply to the named public drop-off routes.

  • Milwaukee food scrap drop-off

    Milwaukee, Wisconsin

    Access
    Sign up through the partner link on the city’s page and choose a listed drop-off. Milwaukee collection guide and sign-up
    Scope
    These are food-scrap carts. Use the city’s separate yard-waste route for grass, branches and leaves. Milwaukee collection guide and sign-up

    Milwaukee collection guide and sign-up

  • Madison Streets Division sites

    Madison, Wisconsin

    Access
    The city limits these drop-off locations to Madison residents and taxpayers. Check site hours and what proof to bring. Madison Streets Division food-scrap guide
    Which bin
    Use the orange food-scrap carts at Streets Division drop-off sites. The page describes farmers’ markets and county kiosks separately. Madison Streets Division food-scrap guide

    Madison Streets Division food-scrap guide

  • Ann Arbor community drop-off bins

    Ann Arbor, Michigan

    Access
    The city currently describes this program for approved residents of multifamily housing. An application by itself does not unlock a bin. Ann Arbor bin access and materials
    Unlocking
    Wait for approval and app instructions. The city describes requesting a separate unlocking method if you cannot use a smartphone. Ann Arbor bin access and materials

    Ann Arbor bin access and materials

  • Minneapolis organics drop-off

    Minneapolis, Minnesota

    Access
    Choose a site and follow the city’s sign-up and welcome instructions to use the locked cart. Minneapolis organics drop-off sites
    Bag
    The drop-off instructions discuss both paper and compostable plastic. Use the linked materials guide to check the BPI mark. Minneapolis accepted organics

    Minneapolis organics drop-off sites

  • Durham city food-scrap containers

    Durham, North Carolina

    Access
    The city describes free residential drop-off for city residents and city employees. Register in CompostHere to unlock the container. Durham city drop-off instructions
    Amount
    The city limits use to two gallons per person per 24-hour or weekend period and excludes commercial and event scraps. Durham city drop-off instructions

    Durham city drop-off instructions

  • Carrboro Town Hall drop-off

    Carrboro, North Carolina

    Location
    The town lists roll-out carts behind Town Hall. The market also posts that its compost drop-off moved there. Check the town’s current directions. Carrboro town drop-off locations and access · Carrboro market’s material list and move notice
    Access
    The town lists an app requirement for the separate BigBelly bins at Jones Ferry and MLK Park. Check the type of bin at the site you plan to use. Carrboro town drop-off locations and access

    Carrboro town drop-off locations and access

Keep the route and material together

One city may offer several kinds of composting. These comparisons cover specific public drop-off routes. Follow the instructions for the bin you are using.
