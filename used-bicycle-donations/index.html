Where to Donate a Used or Broken Bicycle

A community bike shop might repair and rehome a used bicycle, use it for parts or send it to recycling. Start with the exact shop and the condition of your bike.

Milwaukee Bicycle Collective lists bicycles in any condition. Bike New York excludes excessive rust. Full Cycle in Minneapolis describes a suggested donation for severely damaged bikes it has to process.

Before moving a bike from a cleanout

Confirm the owner has marked the bike for donation. Check the current shop hours, then describe any major damage or missing parts before loading an uncertain donation.

Tell the shop if the item is an e-bike or if the battery is separate. This comparison covers the published rules for conventional bicycles. Ask the operator about electric equipment, helmet handling and any gear it does not list.
