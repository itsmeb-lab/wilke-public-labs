Where to Shred Household Documents

Some programs shred documents at an event. Others collect paper in locked bins and shred it later. Check the named route before bringing more than a bag or box.

Orange County, North Carolina takes appointments for a two-box limit and asks you to remove staples and clips. Somerset County events allow staples and clips and let eligible residents watch.

Before you bring documents

Check the appointment or event date, allowed volume, permitted fasteners and residency or membership rule.

  • Orange County Shred Center

    Chapel Hill, North Carolina

    Personal documents
    Book the resident route through Solid Waste. The center offers weekday slots by customer group and says bookings often fill two to four weeks ahead. Orange County Shred Center rules and FAQs
    Container and contents
    Use no more than two boxes at 15 by 12 by 10 inches or smaller. Bring confidential, unshredded paper. Remove staples and clips and leave folders, cards and general mail out. Orange County Shred Center rules and FAQs

    Orange County Shred Center rules and FAQs

  • Lorain County Collection Center

    Elyria, Ohio

    When to go
    The resident collection-center service runs during regular center hours. The county posts separate schedules for community events. Lorain County personal and business shredding rules
    How to prepare the paper
    Staff check for staples, clips, rubber bands and plastic bindings before placing the paper in a locked container. Lorain County personal and business shredding rules

    Lorain County personal and business shredding rules

  • Collier County daily collection bins

    Collier County, Florida

    Named centers
    The county lists the Naples, Marco Island, North Collier and Northeast centers for the daily route. Collier County daily and monthly shredding options
    Volume and charge
    The first 20 pounds per vehicle are free. The county lists $1 per additional pound. Collier County daily and monthly shredding options

    Collier County daily and monthly shredding options

  • Collier County monthly shredding truck

    Collier County, Florida

    Check your center and date
    The county lists this truck on the third Saturday of the month at Naples and North Collier. Check the center page for current hours. Collier County monthly shredding truck route
    Volume and charge
    The first 20 pounds per vehicle are free. The fee is $1 per additional pound under the posted schedule. Collier County monthly shredding truck route

    Collier County monthly shredding truck route

  • Somerset County Operation Secure Shred

    Somerset County, New Jersey

    Before you go
    Check a current county event date and bring proof of a Somerset County address. Somerset County event rules on North Plainfield’s municipal site
    Container and capacity
    The program lists up to six boxes, bags or containers per trip. Collection can end early if the trucks fill. Somerset County event rules on North Plainfield’s municipal site

    Somerset County event rules on North Plainfield’s municipal site

  • UW Credit Union annual member shredding events

    Milwaukee and Madison, Wisconsin

    Who can use it
    These annual events are for UW Credit Union members. Use the organizer’s scheduled date and exact event address. UW Credit Union event dates and member rules
    What to bring
    The organizer lists up to five boxes or bags of qualifying paper. Keep plastic binders, batteries and electronics separate. UW Credit Union event dates and member rules

    UW Credit Union event dates and member rules

Before you load a box

Separate the documents you have decided to shred from papers that still need review. Use the receiving program’s container, fastener and proof-of-eligibility rules.

If you need the papers shredded while you are there, confirm the event’s date and remaining capacity. A locked-bin drop-off can handle paper after your visit.
