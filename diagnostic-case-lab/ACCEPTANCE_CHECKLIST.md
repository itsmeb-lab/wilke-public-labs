## Deliverable completeness

- [ ] The report identifies the **single** agreed question and environment/version.
- [ ] Authorized reproduction steps, inputs and limitations are recorded. If not reproducible, that limit is explicit.
- [ ] Transport response, application result, persisted record/reference and notification/owner acknowledgment are reported separately, where relevant.
- [ ] Each tested scenario has an observed outcome, timestamp/reference and traceable sanitized evidence.
- [ ] Retry, duplicate and failure behavior was tested **where authorized**, or marked not tested with the reason.
- [ ] Findings distinguish confirmed fact, likely cause, alternative hypothesis and unknown.
- [ ] Recommended repair is prioritized and specific enough for a developer to act on.
- [ ] Proposed acceptance tests describe how to know whether a future fix works.
- [ ] Implementation, deployment, external charges and ongoing support were not represented as included.
- [ ] No customer secrets, personal records or sensitive logs are included.
- [ ] Report was delivered through the agreed channel to the named reviewer.
- [ ] The included clarification opportunity and reply route are stated.
