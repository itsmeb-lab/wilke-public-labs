# $150 diagnostic — scope, schedule and delivery runbook

## 1. Qualify without creating an order

Review the customer's non-sensitive brief against the intake template. Ask only for missing details that determine whether a single JavaScript/Node API, webhook or lead-form issue fits. Decline broad audits, emergency remediation, account troubleshooting, new builds or guaranteed repairs. Never promise to recover lost data.

## 2. Written offer and explicit acceptance

Complete this offer record before any investigation:

- Scope reference: [DIAG-YYYYMMDD-###]
- Customer, authorized technical owner and contact channel: [To be confirmed]
- One issue and expected behavior: [Specific statement]
- Included: [Safe reproduction where authorized and feasible; focused tests; prioritized written findings; one clarification]
- Explicitly excluded: [Implementation, production change, monitoring, broad audit, third-party costs, guarantees]
- Permitted environment, access and test effects: [Exact assets and limits]
- Planned start / evidence handoff: [Date + timezone after inputs and agreement]
- Target diagnostic delivery: [Date + timezone agreed in writing, not a blanket public guarantee]
- Clarification method and response window: [Agree in writing]
- Fee: **$150 USD one time**; any tax or payment-processing terms: [Confirm]
- Payment route and timing: [Existing approved Stripe/Square invoice/payment route **only after founder approval and client scope acceptance**; no checkout on current site]
- Confidentiality, artifact retention and deletion: [Agree]
- Acceptance reviewer and contact: [Customer-designated owner]
- Change/suspension rule: [Stop if scope widens, permission is withdrawn, source is unsafe, or required inputs are unavailable]

Customer acceptance must be an affirmative written reply accepting the scope, price, timeframe, access conditions and payment arrangement. An inquiry, click or silence is not acceptance. If the relationship originates on Upwork, use Upwork's contract and payment flow instead of redirecting outside it.

## 3. Payment and scheduling gate

Do not charge, invoice, connect banking or create a public live checkout without the appropriate founder/merchant authorization. Once the terms are accepted and an approved route is ready, record invoice/link reference and payment status separately from scope acceptance; **do not mark paid until processor evidence confirms it**. Begin only under the payment timing agreed in writing.

## 4. Execute one safe diagnosis

1. Record baseline source/version, timezone and authorized environment.
2. Reproduce with synthetic or approved sanitized data; record both transport and application outcome.
3. Trace the handoff to the expected saved record or receiving owner, distinguishing unknown steps.
4. Perform focused positive, negative, retry/duplicate and missing-reference checks only where permitted.
5. Separate observations, probable explanations, alternatives and unresolved unknowns.
6. Form one prioritized repair decision without silently implementing it.
7. Redact and hash evidence where practical. Keep receipts private unless permission explicitly allows sharing.

If reproduction is blocked, deliver a documented limitation and a decision about the next safe test. Do not fabricate a successful reproduction.

## 5. Deliver and request acceptance

Send a concise findings report with the issue, test environment/version, methods, exact evidence, priority, proposed next step, exclusions and unresolved questions. Provide one clarification as agreed. Ask the named reviewer to confirm the report is received and whether the deliverable meets the **documentation** acceptance checklist—not whether a production problem was fixed.

Record: [Delivery timestamp], [Channel/message ID], [Recipient acknowledgment], [Accepted / clarification requested / disputed], [Payment evidence]. Do not infer acceptance from silence. Any implementation must be separately quoted and authorized.

## 6. Close out responsibly

After the agreed review/clarification period, archive only approved sanitized artifacts and the decision record, delete temporary access as agreed, and remove client secrets from transient workspaces. Never publish a customer example, testimonial, revenue claim or detailed case without explicit permission.

This runbook is a reusable draft, not a signed customer contract or an assertion that payment rails are ready.
