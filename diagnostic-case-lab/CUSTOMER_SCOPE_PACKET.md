2. Safely reproduce the issue **if feasible and explicitly authorized** in the named environment; document what cannot be reproduced.
3. Check relevant transport, application, persistence, and notification/owner evidence separately, without claiming unknown steps succeeded.
4. Deliver one written findings report with observations, plausible cause and alternatives, limitations, prioritized repair recommendation and suggested acceptance tests.
5. Provide **one clarification** on that report through the agreed channel and window.

**Not included:** Implementation or production deployment; unlimited debugging; multiple unrelated issues; recovering lost data; emergency incident response; security certification; monitoring; guaranteed resolution or conversion; purchases, third-party fees, or new accounts.
