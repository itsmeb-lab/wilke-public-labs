Bona Traffic HD

U.S. technical data sheet. Confirm the schedule in cooler or more humid rooms.

Walking
The US sheet gives 24 hours for walking. Treat the surface gently while it cures. Bona US technical data sheet
Furniture
Confirm for your floor. Ask the installer for a furniture return time. The sheet gives pad guidance and a separate walking time. Bona US technical data sheet
Rugs and mats
Wait until the finish has fully cured before returning rugs. Bona US technical data sheet
Routine cleaning
Use a dry microfiber mop or cloth during the first week. Confirm the specified cleaner for later use. Bona US technical data sheet
Full cure
The US sheet gives approximately 3 days for full cure. Bona US technical data sheet

LOBA 2K Supra A.T. satin

U.S. satin sheet. Its stated times assume 68 °F and 50% relative humidity at the recommended application rate.

Walking
The sheet lists normal traffic after 12 hours under its stated conditions. LOBA US satin technical data sheet
Furniture
Confirm for your floor. Confirm a separate time for furniture. The care section calls for felt pads and careful placement. LOBA US satin technical data sheet
Rugs and mats
Wait for final cure before setting rugs or carpets on the finish. Check pad compatibility with the installer. LOBA US satin technical data sheet
Routine cleaning
The care section holds off water and detergent for the first week and calls for full cure before damp cleaning. LOBA US satin technical data sheet
Full cure
The US satin sheet lists 3 days under the stated conditions. Ventilation, humidity, temperature and coat thickness can extend it. LOBA US satin technical data sheet

Rubio Monocoat Oil Plus 2C

U.S. guidance for the two-part system. Confirm whether Part B was used.

Walking
The care note gives about 24–36 hours for light use under favorable conditions. The flooring FAQ specifies clean, dry socks for early walking. Rubio US cure-time guidance
Furniture
The flooring FAQ lists 48 hours for furniture. It excludes rugs and mats at that point. Rubio US flooring FAQ
Rugs and mats
Confirm for your floor. The FAQ excludes rugs and mats at the 48-hour furniture milestone. Ask for the actual rug and pad return date. Rubio US flooring FAQ
Routine cleaning
Confirm for your floor. The flooring FAQ lists about 5 days to full cure and liquid resistance. Confirm the first damp cleaning date and product separately. Rubio US flooring FAQ
Full cure
Rubio lists about 5 days with Part B and about 21 days when Part A was used alone. Rubio US cure-time guidance

PALLMANN PALL-X GOLD

July 2026 product data sheet for the North American product.

Walking
Published times differ. The sheet lists 3–4 hours in the drying table and 24 hours in the cure instructions. Get a written walking time from the installer for this floor. PALLMANN July 2026 product data sheet
Furniture
Confirm for your floor. Ask the installer to confirm a separate furniture return time for the installed finish. PALLMANN July 2026 product data sheet
Rugs and mats
Confirm for your floor. Ask when rugs and their actual backing or pads can be placed on this finish. PALLMANN July 2026 product data sheet
Routine cleaning
The cure instructions call for dry untreated dusting or a dry lint-free cloth during the first week. PALLMANN July 2026 product data sheet
Full cure
The July 2026 sheet describes roughly 3 days to full cure under its listed conditions. PALLMANN July 2026 product data sheet

Basic Coatings StreetShoe NXT

Technical sheet for this product with XL Catalyst.

Walking
The technical sheet generally allows light foot traffic after 24 hours. StreetShoe NXT technical data sheet
Furniture
Confirm for your floor. The sheet describes careful furniture placement while the surface can still mar. Confirm the date and handling for your furniture. StreetShoe NXT technical data sheet
Rugs and mats
The sheet calls for at least 2 weeks after the finish has dried before covering it with rugs or carpet. StreetShoe NXT technical data sheet
Routine cleaning
Confirm for your floor. The technical sheet gives dry dusting for the first 7 days and discusses a separate 2-week window for liquid cleaners. Confirm the first damp or cleaner use. StreetShoe NXT technical data sheet
Full cure
The technical sheet describes full cure at about 7 days. StreetShoe NXT technical data sheet

DuraSeal Waterbased Polyurethane

Manufacturer instructions for the waterbased product. Confirm the product name on the label.

Walking
DuraSeal lists 24 hours before light foot traffic. DuraSeal Waterbased Polyurethane instructions
Furniture
Confirm for your floor. Ask for a separate furniture date. The published instructions give a one-week heavy traffic restriction. DuraSeal Waterbased Polyurethane instructions
Rugs and mats
The manufacturer says to wait one week before putting rugs on the finish. DuraSeal Waterbased Polyurethane instructions
Routine cleaning
The manufacturer gives a one-week wait before using cleaners. Check the specified cleaner on the product instructions. DuraSeal Waterbased Polyurethane instructions
Full cure
Confirm for your floor. The instructions give a one-week restriction for rugs, cleaners, spills and heavy traffic. Confirm full cure separately for your floor. DuraSeal Waterbased Polyurethane instructions
