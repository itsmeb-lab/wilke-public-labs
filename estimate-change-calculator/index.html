Example with an added charge

An original estimate of $1,800 plus $75 in earlier approvals is $1,875. A proposed $160 board repair would bring it to $2,035 if approved when all amounts include the same taxes and fees.

What to ask for in writing

  • Scope. Which room, item or quantity changed? Which line on the original estimate does it affect?
  • Price. What is the new charge and what credit applies to the old line? Which amounts include tax and fees?
  • Timing. Which work or finish dates change? What needs approval before the crew proceeds?

If a replacement has one net amount, ask which old line was credited and whether the price already includes that credit. Keep the reply with the existing estimate and any approved changes.
