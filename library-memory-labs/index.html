Digitize VHS and Camcorder Tapes at a Library Memory Lab

Match the exact format printed on your tape before choosing a station. VHS, MiniDV and Hi8 use different equipment. Some labs list the maximum recording length or which tape standard the deck can read.

Greater Victoria accepts MiniDV and Hi8 but requires advance booking. DC lists MiniDV while its Video8, Hi8 and Digital8 equipment is temporarily unavailable. Burnaby welcomes walk-ins when equipment is available.

Before digitizing

Check the media format, first-visit requirements and your storage device for the chosen station.

  • DC Public Library Memory Lab

    Washington, District of Columbia

    Before your first visit
    Complete the participant release and review the library’s guidelines. Bring an active library card and government photo ID. DC Public Library equipment and participant rules
    Book your station
    Use the library’s three-hour booking route. Ask how to save files for your exact format. The 8mm reel equipment uses an SD card with a size limit. DC Public Library equipment and participant rules

    DC Public Library equipment and participant rules

  • Boston Public Library Central Memory Lab

    Boston, Massachusetts

    Before you request a reservation
    Complete the online orientation and use agreement. Boston requires a physical BPL or accepted MBLN card and excludes Minuteman cards and eCards. Boston Central Memory Lab bookings and eligibility
    Choose a station
    VHS uses the audio-video station. Book one station in a three-hour session and wait for a confirmation email. Boston Central Memory Lab bookings and eligibility

    Boston Central Memory Lab bookings and eligibility

  • Los Angeles Public Library DIY Memory Lab

    Los Angeles, California

    Before calling for a reservation
    Have a Los Angeles Public Library card in good standing, complete the online orientation and sign the required agreement. Octavia Lab media specs and reservation rules
    Choose one station
    The lab limits each appointment to one piece of equipment. Ask which storage device to bring for that particular station. Octavia Lab media specs and reservation rules

    Octavia Lab media specs and reservation rules

  • Greater Victoria Public Library at Esquimalt

    Esquimalt, British Columbia

    What to bring
    Bring your library card and a USB or external drive with at least 32 GB of space. Audio users should bring wired headphones. Greater Victoria media lab sessions and equipment
    Where and when
    Use the Esquimalt Branch reservation page. Sessions can be booked for one to three hours in one-hour increments. Greater Victoria media lab sessions and equipment

    Greater Victoria media lab sessions and equipment

  • Coquitlam Public Library Poirier Digitization Station

    Coquitlam, British Columbia

    Before you reserve
    Use your library card to book the Poirier station. The published conversion options do not include PAL-to-NTSC conversion. Coquitlam Poirier supported formats and station guides
    If you want help
    The library provides self-serve instructions. It offers a separate help session for one-on-one orientation. Coquitlam Poirier supported formats and station guides

    Coquitlam Poirier supported formats and station guides

  • Burnaby Public Library Digital Studio

    Burnaby, British Columbia

    Before you travel
    Call to check walk-in equipment availability. Reserve a Studio Station with your library card number and PIN. Burnaby Digital Studio equipment and availability
    What the station supplies
    The hands-on lab may require your own camcorder and compatible cables for some formats. Check the equipment table for your media. Burnaby Digital Studio equipment and availability

    Burnaby Digital Studio equipment and availability

Plan your first session

Choose one test recording before bringing a box of tapes. Ask whether transfer takes as long as the recording and which station handles the format printed on your tape.

Check the library’s file storage rules and leave time to copy and play back the result. Keep the original until you have checked the digital file.
