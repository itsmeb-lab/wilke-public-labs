Choose a removal route

Check who collects trash at the property. A Milwaukee mailing address can have a different municipal collector. Your choices stay on this page.

  • Small load you can move. Check your collector's setout, size and appointment rules. Check facility eligibility before driving a load to a site.
  • Usable items. Ask the donation organization which items and pickup locations it accepts. Milwaukee Habitat ReStore's standard pickup covers eligible items. Its pickup page lists access requirements and a separate paid option.
  • Rooms to empty or a fixed deadline. Get a written cleanout scope and confirm stairs, truck access, disposal, sweep-up and the handoff time.
  • Permission or materials are unclear. Pause those items. Wisconsin DATCP has a landlord and tenant guide for questions about a rental. Check MMSD household hazardous waste eligibility and accepted items before moving chemicals.

Bulky pickup in six Milwaukee-area cities

Start with the city that serves the property. These rules describe municipal routes. A paid hauler should identify where it will take the load and include any charges in its quote.

  • Milwaukee

    City Sanitation serves eligible addresses. Ordinary bulky items up to one cubic yard can go at the regular collection point. Larger eligible loads require a special pickup with a fee. Mixed piles and loads exceeding six cubic yards are excluded from special pickup.

    City bulky rules · City Drop Off Centers

  • West Allis

    The city lists one cubic yard for eligible setout items and extra fees for items over 50 pounds. Queen-size and larger mattresses have a fee. Garbage bags go in the cart.

    West Allis's updated garbage rules

  • Wauwatosa

    Ask Public Works about special collection for furniture or a large cleanout. Items such as TVs and appliances can affect the charge. Confirm what the drop-off center will take before you load.

    Wauwatosa special collection rules

  • Glendale

    Schedule eligible furniture and other bulk items with the city's collector. The city directs residents to set items out the night before the scheduled pickup.

    Glendale bulky item instructions

  • South Milwaukee

    The city lists one no-charge scheduled bulk collection per month for residents. Book in advance and check the current instructions for appliances, carpet and larger pieces.

    South Milwaukee bulk pickup rules

  • City of Brookfield

    Check the city's current paid collection instructions before putting furniture out. The city recycling center takes appliances under its rules. It excludes other bulky items.

    City of Brookfield bulky rules

For a TV, appliance, mattress or mixed load, see the Milwaukee and Wauwatosa item-by-item guide.

Write the scope before asking for a price

Copy this list or print it for the walk-through. Use the same room list, load sizes and access details for every estimate.

Rooms and contents
List the rooms to empty, large items and anything clearly marked to stay. For an estate or rental, confirm who will approve any disputed items before removal.
Access
Count the flights of stairs and note tight turns and the distance to the loading spot. Confirm who will let the crew in.
Charges
Ask how much volume the price includes, what an extra load costs, and which disposal, lifting or sweep-up charges are separate.
Handoff
Put the completion deadline and expected room condition in writing. Decide who will confirm the job is done and whether photos are needed.
