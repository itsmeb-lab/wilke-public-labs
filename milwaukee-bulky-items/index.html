Can a donation pickup move furniture down stairs?

Pickup options reviewed November 25, 2026.

  • ReStore standard pickup

    Milwaukee Habitat ReStore offers free pickup for eligible items. The published route requires a first-floor location, a garage or a building with elevator access. Its donation center excludes mattresses and box springs.

    ReStore pickup rules · ReStore item rules

  • ReStore’s paid pickup partner

    ReSupply’s paid route handles stairs. Eligible items go to ReStore. Ask the pickup provider for the current quote and where any remaining items will go.

    ReStore partner pickup rules

  • Goodwill’s furniture pickup partners

    Goodwill Greater Milwaukee & Chicago does not take furniture directly. Its pickup partners can collect furniture and route it to other nonprofits. Check the partner’s charges, access rules and service area before booking.

    Goodwill donation and partner rules
