Do you have to return seeds to a seed library?

Some seed libraries let you take seeds without a library card. Others require a card or registration. A library can ask you to bring back unused seeds, invite you to share seeds from your harvest, or make returns optional.

Choose a question and check the named library. The comparison covers published rules and pickup steps. Check stock with the library before making a trip.

Before you pick up seeds

Find the program’s pickup location, then look for its sign-out, registration or donation steps.

  • Transylvania County Library

    Brevard, North Carolina

    Visit
    Find the seed collection on the first floor. Take what you plan to grow. Transylvania County Seed Library

    Transylvania County Seed Library

  • Canton Seed Library

    Canton, North Carolina

    Bring your card
    Bring a Cardinal library card in good standing. The seed library follows the branch’s open hours. Haywood County seed library instructions

    Haywood County seed library instructions

  • Cleveland County Library System

    Shelby and Lawndale, North Carolina

    Check out
    Complete the seed checkout slip and put it in the card box. The page lists the Eugenia H. Young Memorial and Spangler libraries. Cleveland County Seed Library

    Cleveland County Seed Library

  • State Library of North Carolina

    Raleigh, North Carolina

    Sign out
    Pick up seeds in person during onsite hours. Sign out your selections for the library’s inventory. State Library native seed program

    Middle Island, New York

    Pick up
    Help yourself to the packets. Take what you can use this season. Longwood Grows policy
