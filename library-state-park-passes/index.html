Borrow a State or Provincial Park Pass from a Public Library

A library pass can cover the vehicle day-use fee. Start by checking which library lends it, which parks accept it and what the park needs from you when you arrive.

York County Library in South Carolina lends its passport for a week but takes no holds. Toronto also lends its Ontario pass for a week and lets you place a hold. Parks can still reach capacity before you arrive.

Plan the two parts of your visit

Confirm the library’s pickup and return terms. A statewide page may describe park access without telling you whether your branch takes holds or has a pass available.

Check the park itself before traveling. Some parking lots fill and some parks require their own day-use permit. A day-use pass typically covers a different fee from a campsite, tour or equipment rental.
