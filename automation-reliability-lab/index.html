What this proves

Four controls buyers should be able to inspect, not just hear about.

  1. Deterministic recovery

    Bounded retry and explicit fallback instead of silent failure or endless retry loops.

  2. Approval before consequence

    Gated actions cannot dispatch until a human decision exists.

  3. Idempotency

    Replaying the same completed request is suppressed rather than duplicated.

  4. Auditability

    Every meaningful transition emits a timestamped receipt you can download and inspect.
