Milwaukee cleanout to hardwood estimate checklist

Use this when one crew is clearing a room and a flooring contractor needs to see what is underneath. Decide what stays first, then get a written estimate for each job.

Use your browser’s print menu for a blank worksheet. Fill it in offline and keep access codes out of the copy you share.

Before the cleanout

  • Confirm who can approve removal. List the rooms and the items the owner or authorized representative has cleared for removal. Pause on disputed, occupied or unapproved areas. For belongings left by a tenant, review the Wisconsin landlord and tenant guide before authorizing removal.
  • Separate keep, move and remove. Label retained items and decide who will move them. Record stairs, loading access, unusual items and whether the quote includes sweeping.
  • Choose the receiving route. For an eligible curb item, check the Milwaukee and Wauwatosa bulky item guide. For usable furniture, check pickup eligibility and stairs rules. If a hauler is removing a load, ask where it will go.

Before approving the flooring estimate

  • Confirm the rooms are accessible. Ask the flooring contractor whether the inspection can proceed while any furniture or covering remains. Record visible damage and what the contractor still needs to examine.
  • Compare the same treatment. Ask whether the estimate covers a maintenance coat, sanding and refinishing, or repair before finishing. Compare the same rooms, repairs, finish and exclusions. NWFA refinishing overview
  • Leave time for the finish. Ask the contractor to put inspection, start, completion, and return-to-use dates in writing. Get separate instructions for foot traffic and moving furniture or rugs back in. Wisconsin home improvement guidance
