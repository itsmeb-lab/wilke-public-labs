Where can I borrow a home energy meter or thermal camera?

Start with what you want to measure. A plug-in meter measures a compatible appliance's electricity use. A thermal camera produces a heat image. A spot infrared thermometer gives a temperature reading for one area at a time.

These eight programs publish lending information. Check the exact item and pickup location with the library. The policy below describes the named loan and its published rules. Catalog stock can change at any time.

What to put in a hold request

Include the tool or kit name, a preferred pickup branch and when you can collect it. Ask staff to confirm the exact device, card or ID requirements, the due date and how to return the equipment. For a camera, check whether you also need a phone, an app or a charging cable.
