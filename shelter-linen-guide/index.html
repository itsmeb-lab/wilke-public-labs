Can I donate towels, sheets or blankets to an animal shelter?

Choose the linen you have. Compare what selected shelters list and check the drop-off instructions before loading the car.

Call a shelter if its wish list leaves your size, material or quantity unclear. Rules can change as a shelter fills up or runs low.

How to drop off a donation

Check the current wish list, drop-off hours and any instructions for bags or bulk quantities. Confirm that your items are clean and in the condition the organization accepts.

  • Wisconsin Humane Society

    Milwaukee and other Wisconsin campuses, US

    Where to go
    Use the wish list’s campus directory to confirm the appropriate drop-off location. Wisconsin Humane Society wish list
    Condition
    The wish list asks for new or gently used towels. Wisconsin Humane Society wish list

    Wisconsin Humane Society wish list

  • Humane Society of Charlotte

    Charlotte, North Carolina, US

    Where to go
    Bring in-person donations to the Pet Help Center and give them to staff. Keep items with you until the center can receive them. Humane Society of Charlotte donation guide
    Large donations
    Arrange an appointment with the organization before bringing a large quantity. Humane Society of Charlotte donation guide

    Humane Society of Charlotte donation guide

  • Haywood County Animal Shelter

    Clyde, North Carolina, US

    Where to go
    The county directs donations to the bench outside its intake area. Follow the published drop-off directions. Haywood County shelter donation guide
    Receipt
    The county directs people who need a receipt to visit during regular business hours. Haywood County shelter donation guide

    Haywood County shelter donation guide

  • Animal Orphanage

    Voorhees, New Jersey, US

    Where to go
    Check the wish list and current visitor information before bringing a donation. Animal Orphanage wish list
    Sheets and towels
    The wish list groups several kinds of bedding together. Ask about shape, size or condition if your item is unclear. Animal Orphanage wish list

    Animal Orphanage wish list

  • SPCA Christchurch Centre

    Christchurch, Canterbury, New Zealand

    Where to go
    The Christchurch page directs linen, towels and blankets for animal care to the centre during opening hours. SPCA Christchurch donation instructions
    Channel
    Use the centre’s donation route for pet bedding and its separate Op Shop instructions for goods intended for resale. SPCA Christchurch donation instructions

    SPCA Christchurch donation instructions

  • Casa Grande Animal Care and Adoption Center

    Casa Grande, Arizona, US

    Where to go
    Use the address and contact information on the city’s current donation guide. Casa Grande Animal Care donation guide
    Condition
    The city excludes heavily used and unwashed donations. Casa Grande Animal Care donation guide

    Casa Grande Animal Care donation guide

  • Humane Society Silicon Valley

    Milpitas, California, US

    Where to go
    Use the marked donation bins at the shelter during its published business hours. Humane Society Silicon Valley donation guide
    Condition
    The shelter asks for clean, gently used linens. Humane Society Silicon Valley donation guide

    Humane Society Silicon Valley donation guide
