Can I borrow tools from a tool library?

A tool library may lend to individual residents, require a paid membership, or serve registered community organizations. Check who can borrow and how fees work for the specific route.

A program can be free to join and still charge for a loan. A paid program can also include most loans in its membership. Confirm availability and the policy for your exact item.

Check your particular tool and job

Use the program’s own checkout and return instructions. Keep any tool-specific loan period or condition with the quote or reservation. Hardware, batteries and consumables can be handled separately.

For a community group, use the organizer route. The Charlotte ToolBank instructions describe how member agencies order, pick up and return tools.
