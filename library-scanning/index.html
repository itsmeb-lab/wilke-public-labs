Pick the scan you need and compare seven libraries. Check where you can send the file, whether you need a card and what size the scanner takes.

Check with your branch for equipment availability. Some locations charge per page for USB scanning or offer USB without direct email.

Choose the output before scanning

Bring a USB drive if the branch supports that output. If scanning to email, check the address and open the attachment before leaving. Take every original and check that the file includes all sides in the right order.

For a large sheet or a book, ask staff whether to use the flatbed. For a stack of loose pages, confirm the feeder and whether you can scan both sides in one pass.
